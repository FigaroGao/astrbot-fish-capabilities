## Enabling this in your own installation

All new shared feature switches default to off, and the running configuration is not included. Configure the owner-bound private session and existing services first, then enable `private_initiative_enabled`, `timetable_enabled`, `autonomy_enabled`, `owner_notes_enabled`, and `meme_wiki_enabled` as needed. Autonomous access to the owner's public shelf requires separate permission through `bili_owner_shelf_autonomous_enabled` and the authorized owner's UID. Search, match records, weather, the shop, and Bilibili also require their own service switches and credentials. Enable `bili_scroll_unlimited_enabled` for model-selected viewing quantities; the per-session time window still applies.

The reference configuration uses `initiative_start_hour=11`, `initiative_end_hour=4`, `initiative_interval_minutes=10`, and `private_recent_conversation_seconds=120`. The autonomous controller's completion-relative cooldown is fixed at 600 seconds. The interval field remains in use by existing activity services; it is not a separate adjustment for this controller's cooldown. After confirming the times, reload the plugin to migrate the old default tasks. Restarting QQ is unnecessary.

Users must obtain and verify `data/memes.db` themselves from [upstream](https://github.com/thawline/dsh-memewiki/tree/ffbc4ceae61bbb117994c0c9271b6452c96c2455), then place it at `plugin_data/astrbot_plugin_fish_capabilities/memewiki/memes.db` under AstrBot's data directory. Keep upstream NOTICE and LICENSE in the same folder. Running its Node plugin is unnecessary. A missing corpus returns `dictionary_not_installed`. Check the source and applicable terms; this repository's license does not replace the rights conditions of the corpus contents.

## Timing and actions

Autonomous decisions are allowed from 11:00 to 04:00 the next day, configured local time. The bot rests from 04:00 to 11:00 but continues to respond normally when the owner initiates a conversation. The existing nightly review remains at 05:00, with its original recent-conversation avoidance rule.

The unified timetable polls the controller every minute, but does not call the model every minute. The controller persists the completion timestamp and waits at least 600 seconds after the entire activity finishes before the next decision, including across reloads. It skips this decision when the owner has chatted within the last 120 seconds. Scheduler granularity can make the actual wait slightly longer than ten minutes.

The model selects one activity and can use the result to choose a few related follow-up activities: at most three distinct actions, within a fifteen-minute execution window. It may finish quietly; speaking is not required in each cycle. Choices include browsing Bilibili, reading the owner's public likes or favorites, recalling memories, writing private notes, chatting or asking a question, searching public knowledge, checking configured-city weather or the daily VALORANT shop, quietly syncing matches, looking up a meme, or reviewing notes.

Autonomous weather and shop checks each allow at most one attempt per configured local calendar day. Failed attempts are not repeatedly retried that day. Explicit owner queries continue through the existing tools and do not consume this autonomous allowance. Contacting the owner still uses the original busy-state checks, topic deduplication, unanswered-message suppression, and pre-send checks. The old two-messages-per-day and three-hour limits were not restored.

A SQLite lease and an in-process lock prevent duplicate activities. Cancellation and errors also record a completion timestamp and enter cooldown; an abandoned lease expires. Only bounded result excerpts from the last 96 cycles are retained, rather than complete subtitles, images, or reasoning.

## Bilibili and matches

The old system-default browsing tasks at 12:30, 16:30, and 20:30 are paused in favor of autonomous activity selection. Owner-created tasks are not deleted. After each actual video read, the model decides whether to continue, change the public topic, or stop. It no longer relies on its pre-reading intention to continue. Existing per-session time windows, maximum video duration, and public-post review remain in place.

The owner has continuously authorized public likes and favorites as private selection hints, so a fresh request to view favorites is unnecessary each time. Only public folders are read and ownership is verified. A favorite does not imply endorsement; the shelf itself is not turned into a permanent personality conclusion or public-comment material. Independently discovered videos can become private conversation topics: express a view and discuss the other person's thoughts instead of mechanically introducing material they have already seen. Answer honestly when asked about the source.

New matches are quietly synced every ten minutes during the activity window. The first run establishes a baseline; subsequent runs deduplicate by `match_ref` without sending a QQ message for every match. A finite sample of recent matches is not real-time gaming presence, and numerous matches between polls may not all be captured. Candidate topics still require a suitability decision, without demanding reports or applying guilt.

## Private notebook

The independent `owner-notebook.private.sqlite3` database is accessed through `fish_owner_notebook`, available only in the bound owner's private session. Types are `correction`, `knowledge_gap`, `impression`, and `personal_note`. Impressions are explicitly subjective inferences, not objective facts or psychological diagnoses.

Writing requires a contiguous quote from currently obtained source material. A revision references the previous ID, deactivates the older version, and preserves its audit history. Archiving is a soft deletion. This notebook is not injected into group conversations or public comments. Structured storage does not establish truth; the model must still record cautiously and follow evidence.

The companionship addendum only adds that Xiaojing likes her owner most, together with boundaries against demanding responses or reports and permission to retain her own interests. The main owner-defined persona was not replaced.

## Meme lookup and public search

This implementation references [thawline/dsh-memewiki](https://github.com/thawline/dsh-memewiki) without installing its DSH-specific Node plugin. The 6,668-entry SQLite corpus is placed in the local private data directory with upstream NOTICE and LICENSE; it is not included in the public source. Upstream attributes the data to cnmeme.wiki and credits the original AstrBot plugin author in1t1a1. The wrapper's license is not treated as redistribution permission for every encyclopedia entry.

`fish_meme_wiki` returns one bounded entry per lookup. Short bare messages require an exact title match, preventing dictionary material from being forced into every conversation. Entries may be outdated or disputed; offensive nicknames are not recommended vocabulary.

Public searches reuse the configured official DeepSeek search service. Only a separate public-topic query is sent, with an additional model check for private information and no chat history. Credentials and private clues are blocked. Arbitrary URL fetching and automatic retries are not allowed. The search service is billable, and a summary does not establish a verified fact.
